### Orders scribe

Q: What is the difference between Direct Orders and Third-Party Orders in Simply Depo, and when should I use a Third-Party Order? 

A: Understanding the two order types is crucial for proper fulfillment:

* Direct Orders: These are standard day-to-day transactions placed directly between your business and your customer. You handle all aspects of the order, including the products, pricing, delivery, payment terms, and invoicing.
